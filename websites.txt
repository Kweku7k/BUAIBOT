CORPORATE AFFAIRS & PR
About Us
The Corporate Affairs and Public Relations Directorate falls under the Registry of the University, and is primarily responsible for planning, organising and managing programmes and activities which enhance the flow of communication and the dissemination of information between the University and its publics.
The schedules of the Directorate are made up of the following:
Media relations
University publications
Official functions
Public Relations support for management
Information/communication, granting permission for filming, photography and hoisting of banners
Granting permission for posting to be done on the University website
Protocol assistance and tourism
Managing the university website

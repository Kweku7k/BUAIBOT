AI Training Documentation: Central University Student Account Support Guide
💼
System Overview
Students at Central University use three main platforms for academic and administrative tasks:
Student Email
: For communication and official notices.
Student Portal (OSIS)
: For academic records, results, and course registration.
VCampus
: For e-learning and virtual classes.
The AI model must assist students in:
Creating accounts
Modifying account info
Resetting forgotten passwords
Accessing locked accounts
1.
🆕
Student Account Creation
Step-by-Step: Creating a New Account
Open your browser
(Chrome or Safari recommended).
Go to
https://student.central.edu.gh/
//scroll down
Click on
“Create an account now”
if you don't already have one.
Fill in the registration form:
Full name
Student and personal email
Phone number
Password & confirm password
Level, campus, school, department, session
Click
Submit
to complete the process.
You can now log in using your credentials.
—> Sometime when the submit button is not working, you need to retype your password and make sure all the requirements are met (ie. the password matches, it is green, and every other field is filled)
/click on create student email
2.
🔁
Modifying Student Account Information
How to Update Account Details:
Visit the portal:
https://student.central.edu.gh/login
Log in with your student email and password.
Navigate to
Profile Settings
.
Modify information such as phone number, personal email, department, etc.
Click
Save Changes
.
3.
🔐
Password Reset Instructions
🔧
A. Resetting Your
Student Email Password
Open your browser and visit
https://student.central.edu.gh/
Click on
“Reset Password.”
Enter your
student email
(e.g.,
int18010252@central.edu.gh
).
A
reset link
will be sent to your
personal email
.
Open your personal email inbox (or
check your spam folder
).
Click on the reset link to create a new password.
🔧
B. Resetting Your
OSIS Portal Password
Open your browser and go to:
Student Dashboard:
https://forms.central.edu.gh/student/dashboard
OR directly to OSIS login:
https://osissip.osis.online/auth/login
Click on
“Forgotten Password”
.
On the new page, enter your
student email
.
Complete the
CAPTCHA
to verify you're not a robot.
Click
Reset
.
A reset link will be sent to your
student email
.
If it's not in your inbox, check the
spam folder
.
Open the email, click on the reset link, and follow the steps to set your new OSIS password.
After resetting, you'll be redirected to your dashboard.
🔧
C. Resetting Your
VCampus Password
Go to the VCampus login page (usually linked from student portal).
Log in using:
Username
: Your
Index Number
(e.g.,
int18010252
)
Password
: Default is
"Central@123"
if you've never logged in.
Click on
“Forgot Password.”
A reset link will be sent to your
student email
.
If not found in the inbox,
check your spam folder
.
Click on the reset link in your email.
Enter a new password and click
“Save Changes.”
4.
🚫
Troubleshooting Login Issues
Common Issues & Solutions:
Incorrect Password
: Follow the reset steps.
Didn’t Receive Reset Email
: Check spam or try resending the request.
Email Doesn’t Exist/Error
: Make sure you're entering the
correct format
of the student email (e.g.,
int18010252@central.edu.gh
).
Locked Out
: Wait a few minutes and try again. If locked for too long, contact support.

Notes for AI Training
When training the AI model, ensure it can:
Recognize different platforms (Email vs OSIS vs VCampus)
Ask the user clarifying questions (e.g., "Which platform are you trying to access?")
Guide through precise steps with URLs
Offer fallback options like spam folder checks or IT support contact
Detect and prevent confusion between personal and student email usage
